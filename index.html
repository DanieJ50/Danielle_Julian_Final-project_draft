      📖 About CCD

      Comfort food, redesigned through a personal creative system.

      Cozy Core Diet (CCD) is the recipe world behind Berry Vibes Studio. The project treats food discovery like an interface: recipes become cards, cravings become navigation paths, and comparisons become playful design moments.

      The goal is not to make food feel clinical. It is to make familiar comfort feel expressive, visual, and interactive.
