About Cozy Core Diet

Comfort food, remixed with creativity

CCD is Danielle’s personal recipe world: cozy, flexible, playful, and built around familiar foods. The goal is not to label one choice as “good” and another as “bad.” It is to explore flavor, texture, convenience, and creativity through choices that still feel comforting.
